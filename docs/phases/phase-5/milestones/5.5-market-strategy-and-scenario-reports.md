# Milestone 5.5: Market Strategy And Scenario Reports

Status: Complete

## Goal

Generate market strategy research notes that frame scenarios and watch items without giving direct trade instructions.

## Deliverables

- Market strategy report template.
- Scenario framing for base, upside, and downside cases.
- Watch items for rates, credit, breadth, sectors, and macro releases.
- Guardrail-safe language for strategy and targets.
- Tests for scenario sections and price-target guardrails.

## Implementation Notes

- Added manual market strategy report generation through `POST /api/v1/reports/market-strategy`.
- Reused the Phase 4 agent query pipeline with `market_research` intent.
- Saved generated market strategy reports with confidence, guardrail status, metadata, citations, and expected scenario sections.
- Added a Dashboard UI action and in-session market strategy report preview.
- The prompt asks for base, upside, and downside scenario framing while avoiding direct buy, sell, or hold instructions.

## Definition Of Done

- A user can generate a market strategy note from current evidence.
- The report provides research context and scenarios, not direct recommendations.
- Citations and risks are visible.
