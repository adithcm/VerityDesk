# Milestone 5.4: Company Research Report Generator

Status: Planned

## Goal

Generate company and ticker-focused reports using filings, news, market context, and risk evidence.

## Deliverables

- Company report request fields for tickers, company names, and time window.
- Company research template.
- Evidence selection for filings, company news, sector context, and macro risks.
- Citation-backed catalyst and risk sections.
- Tests for ticker filtering and safe investment language.

## Definition Of Done

- A user can request a company report for a ticker or company name.
- The report separates facts, interpretations, risks, and open questions.
- The report does not tell the user to buy, sell, or hold.
