# Milestone 5.6: Housing And Macro Reports

Status: Planned

## Goal

Generate housing and macro reports that combine news, rates, labor, credit, mortgage, and housing data.

## Deliverables

- Housing report template.
- Macro report template.
- Evidence selection for FRED, Census, FHFA, Freddie Mac, and relevant news.
- Affordability, rates, supply, demand, and labor-market sections.
- Tests for macro and housing evidence packages.

## Definition Of Done

- A user can generate housing and macro reports.
- Official economic observations are labeled clearly and grouped cleanly.
- The report states data recency and uncertainty.
