Status: Planned

- Consolidate profile management.
- Consolidate source/API status.

- Source health cards/drawers.
- Watch item management.
- Schedule/profile controls.
- Refresh controls.

## Manual Steps

None expected.

## Validation

- Home stays simple.
- Settings exposes all operational controls needed for debugging and setup.
