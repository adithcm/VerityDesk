# 9.6.8 Settings Consolidation

Status: Complete

## Goal

Move complexity out of Home and into Settings.

## Scope

- Consolidate profile management.
- Consolidate source/API status.
- Consolidate refresh controls.
- Consolidate watch items.
- Consolidate daily brief schedule.
- Consolidate provider/key/model status.
- Keep advanced technical details collapsible.

## Deliverables

- Settings layout matching Option M visual direction.
- Source health cards/drawers.
- Watch item overview and evaluation action.
- Schedule/profile controls.
- Refresh controls.
- Saved report history, latest retrieved items, recent runs, and provider status moved into Settings.

## Implementation Notes

- Home remains limited to visual modules and one ask bar.
- Settings now centralizes operational information that was previously spread across separate top-level pages.
- Advanced raw ingestion details are collapsed by default.

## Manual Steps

None expected.

## Validation

- Home stays simple.
- Settings exposes all operational controls needed for debugging and setup.
