# Milestone 4.5: Citations And Guardrails

Status: Planned

## Goal

Make agent responses source-grounded and safe for investment-adjacent use.

## Deliverables

- `CitationAgent` or citation enrichment service.
- Citation formatting with source name, URL, published date, and retrieved date.
- `RiskGuardrailAgent` integration.
- Required disclaimer for investing-oriented outputs.
- Checks that block direct buy/sell instructions, guaranteed-return language, and unsupported price targets.
- Tests for citation coverage and guardrail enforcement.

## Definition Of Done

- Investment-related answers include disclaimers and risk framing.
- Responses cite their evidence.
- Unsafe recommendations are blocked or rewritten.
